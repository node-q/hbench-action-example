Harbour log, week one. The ferry left at dawn and the ferry came back at dusk.
The gulls followed the ferry; the gulls always follow the ferry when the nets are full.
Nets were mended on Tuesday. On Wednesday the wind turned, and the boats stayed in.
Thursday: the ferry ran late, the market opened late, and the gulls were early.
Friday the nets came up full again. Full nets, full market, a full harbour.
Saturday the ferry did not run. The harbour was quiet; even the gulls were quiet.
Sunday: mended the nets, painted the boats, watched the ferry come in at last.
